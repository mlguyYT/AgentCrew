# Support Triage Checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist when turning a support report, customer issue, or bug ticket into actionable AgentCrew work.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] customer-sensitive data, secrets, raw logs, personal identifiers, and private paths are removed
- [ ] report is summarized in neutral, factual language
- [ ] severity, impact, urgency, and confidence are recorded
- [ ] affected workflow, environment, version, and reproduction evidence are captured when available
- [ ] confirmed facts are separated from assumptions
- [ ] open questions are blockers only
- [ ] next owner is clear: Developer, Tester, Product Manager, Documentation Agent, Security Reviewer, Release Manager, or Human
- [ ] security, privacy, payment, data-loss, outage, compliance, or public-impact risk is escalated
- [ ] customer response or commitment remains human-approved when needed

---

## Severity Options

```yaml
severity:
  - critical
```
