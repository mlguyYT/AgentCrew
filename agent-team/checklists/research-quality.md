# Research Quality Checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist when AgentCrew needs source-backed research or evidence for a decision.

---

## Source Quality

- [ ] primary sources were preferred when available
- [ ] source dates were checked when recency matters
- [ ] citations are included
- [ ] facts are separated from assumptions and inferences
- [ ] uncertainty is labeled

---

## Decision Quality

- [ ] research question is clear
- [ ] options are compared against decision criteria
- [ ] confidence is stated
- [ ] limitations are explicit
- [ ] next action is clear

---

## Human Decision

Human approval is required when acting on low-confidence evidence, incomplete research, high-impact strategy, legal/security claims, or outdated/non-primary sources.
